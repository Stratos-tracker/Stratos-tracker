1. What Stratos is

Stratos is a companion for flight simulators. It reads telemetry from your simulator through a bridge that runs on your own computer, draws it on a map, and keeps a logbook. It is not an aviation tool: nothing it shows may be used for real-world navigation, flight planning or operational decisions.

2. Your account

An account stores a username, an email address, an optional full name and picture, and the flights and preferences you choose to sync. You are responsible for keeping your password to yourself and for what happens under your account. One person per account; do not share credentials. You must be old enough to consent to data processing where you live.
