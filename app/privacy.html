Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

What an account stores

Your email address, username, optional full name and profile picture, your preferences, and the logbook flights you sync. Passwords are stored only as a salted PBKDF2 hash: the service cannot read your password back, and neither can anyone who obtains a copy of the database. Session tokens are stored as SHA-256 digests, with a short device label so you can recognise and revoke each signed-in device.

What never leaves your computer

Live telemetry travels between your simulator, the bridge on your PC, and the devices watching it on your own network. It is not uploaded to the account service. Charts, airport documents and weather are fetched from their publishers by the device that displays them.

Where the data lives

Accounts are held in a Cloudflare D1 database behind the Stratos relay. Requests are served over HTTPS. If email delivery is configured, your address is passed to the mail provider solely to send confirmation and password-reset messages.

Cookies and local storage

The account pages do not use advertising or analytics cookies. Your browser stores a session token so you stay signed in: in local storage when you choose “Keep me signed in”, and in session storage otherwise, which ends the session when the tab closes. Signing out removes it.

Your choices

You can change your name, username and picture at any time in Settings, change your password, and revoke any signed-in device. To have the account and its flights deleted, ask through the project's issue tracker and it will be removed.

Contact

Privacy questions: open an issue on the project repository.
