Sign in to your flight deck.

Stratos keeps your logbook, aircraft and flight hours together, then puts them on the map you fly from — PC, tablet or phone.

  • Your logbook follows you. Every flight the bridge records appears on each signed-in device.
  • Telemetry stays local. Live position moves between your PC and your bridge; the account only carries the summary.
  • You stay in control. Any device can be signed out from Settings, and a password reset signs them all out.
